Privacy Policy

Earth Report Publisher · last updated 14 November 2026

Earth Report Publisher is used only by the Earth Report team for its own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone else.

What we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and the account's basic profile (open id, display name) so it can post our videos to that account. It uses the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish for nothing else.

Storage

Tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in our own systems, never published, and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's settings (Security, Manage app permissions). Videos are our own files.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: open an issue at github.com/addvaluewithai-hub/vertex_ai.
